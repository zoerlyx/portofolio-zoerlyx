SHOOT THE
PUMPKIN

Players navigate a 360-degree FPS environment to shoot randomly spawning pumpkins within a fixed time limit. To maintain a high score, players must stay vigilant and avoid shooting non-target objects like black rabbits and bats, which apply score penalties.

THE FEATURES & VISION

Dual Difficulty Modes

Easy and hard levels with distinct map layouts and scaling challenges.

360° FPS Camera

Immersive first-person controls for dynamic target tracking.

Precision Scoring System

Real-time score calculation with built-in time limits and target penalty mechanics.

Project Goal

Developed as a client project for psychological research to measure and evaluate players' cognitive focus, reaction time, and visual attention.
